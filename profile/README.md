## Projects

### PMOC Pronto
A free generator for the **PMOC** (Plano de Manutenção, Operação e Controle), following Annex I of Brazilian Ordinance GM/MS nº 3.523/1998.

Fill in the establishment, responsible parties, rooms and equipment, and get a complete plan with an annual schedule and 12 monthly record sheets, ready to print or save as PDF.

- Free, no signup required
- Runs entirely in your browser; your data is never sent to a server
- Print-ready output
